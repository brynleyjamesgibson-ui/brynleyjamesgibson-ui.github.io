About

23 years in games. Executive Producer. Operator.

For 23 years Brynley Gibson has worked in video games and for over half that time has held the position of Executive Producer or equivalent. He is as comfortable in the C-suite as he is on the dev floor.

His career has taken him across AAA and AA, corporations and independents, from first party to work for hire to publishing. He has worked at studios including Lionhead, Sony London, Avalanche Studios Group and Sharkmob. He has produced projects for Sony PlayStation, Tencent, Nintendo, Activision and Microsoft amongst many others.
